OUR PROCESS

From an idea to a work of art.

Every Chitrabaddh artwork is created with patience, creativity and attention to detail. From the first idea to the final finishing touch, every step matters.

01

Inspiration

Every artwork begins with an idea. Inspiration comes from Indian traditions, nature, culture and the beauty of everyday life. Colours, patterns and cultural elements help us create meaningful handmade artworks.

02

Sketching

The initial idea is carefully transformed into a detailed sketch. Shapes, compositions and small details are planned before painting begins.

03

Creating

Colours, textures and artistic techniques are brought together to create the artwork by hand. Each piece receives individual attention.

04

Finishing

Once the artwork is complete, every detail is carefully checked. Final touches are added to make the artwork ready for your space.

05

Ready to Inspire

The finished artwork is carefully prepared and ready to become a meaningful part of someone's home, office or special space.
