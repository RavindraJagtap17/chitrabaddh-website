OUR STORY

Where art meets emotion.

Chitrabaddh is a celebration of handmade art, inspired by Indian traditions, nature and everyday beauty.

Every artwork is thoughtfully created with love, patience and attention to detail, bringing stories and emotions to your walls.

Our aim is to create meaningful artworks that add warmth, beauty and personality to every space.
